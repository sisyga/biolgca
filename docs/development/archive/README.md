# Archive

Finished plans, specs, reviews and reports. They describe how the current code
came about and why; they are not updated. Open items from them are in the
[roadmap](../roadmap.md). Files are named by the date the work started.

## Plans

- `plans/2026-08-21-aidevelop-review-and-hardening.md`,
  `plans/2026-08-21-release-readiness-follow-up.md`: hardening of the
  `aidevelop` branch before the usability work.
- `plans/2026-08-30-teaching-notebooks.md`: the tutorial notebooks.
- `plans/2026-09-23-usability-roadmap.md`: the usability plan (phases 0 to 4:
  rules as decorated functions, one mechanism for all model families, sweeps,
  model files, the Explorer, the zoo, fields), with every dated status note
  and decision up to 2026-10-01.

## Specs

- `specs/2026-08-30-teaching-notebooks-design.md`: design of the tutorials.
- `specs/2026-09-25-fields-spec.md`: the `pde` operator, its solvers and how
  they were built, including the nonlinear solver (Newton's method) and its
  measurements.
- `specs/2026-09-26-zoo-spec.md`: the model zoo, its eight entries and how
  each was built.

## Reviews

- `reviews/2026-09-30-code-review.md`: the code review of 30 September, its
  fixes and the follow-up review.
- `reviews/2026-09-30-architecture-proposals.md`: the designs, measurements
  and decisions for its architectural findings (rule contracts and rollback,
  the nonlinear field solver, configuration ownership and provenance), and
  how each was built.

## Reports

- `reports/milestone-1-validation.md`, `reports/milestone-1-reopened-validation.md`,
  `reports/plotting-milestone-c.md`: validation and benchmark reports of early
  milestones; they refer to scripts and results in `benchmarks/`.
